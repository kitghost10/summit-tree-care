Services

Complete arboricultural services

Every engagement begins with an arborist's assessment — not a sales pitch. We recommend only the work your trees actually need.

01

Tree Removal & Hazard Mitigation

Sectional dismantling and precision rigging for dead, diseased, storm-damaged, or structurally compromised trees — including crane-assisted removals in confined spaces, with full protection of surrounding structures and plantings.

  • Crane removals
  • Hazard assessment
  • Insurance documentation
02

Structural Pruning & Trimming

ANSI A300-standard pruning that builds strong architecture, removes deadwood, restores clearance, and reduces storm risk — performed by trained climbers, never topped, never spiked on live trees.

  • Crown cleaning & reduction
  • Deadwood removal
  • Clearance pruning
03

Plant Health Care

Diagnosis and treatment programs for insects, disease, and nutrient deficiencies — soil analysis, deep-root fertilization, and targeted, minimal-impact treatments prescribed by a certified arborist.

  • Disease diagnosis
  • Soil & root care
  • Fertilization programs
04

Stump Grinding & Site Restoration

Stumps ground 6–12 inches below grade, surface roots addressed, and the site backfilled, graded, and finished — ready for turf, planting beds, or a replacement tree selected with our arborists' guidance.

  • Deep grinding
  • Root tracing
  • Replanting advice
05

Storm Response & Emergency Service

When weather turns, our crews are on call around the clock — clearing trees from roofs, driveways, and access routes safely, and documenting damage for your insurer.

  • 24/7 call-out
  • Roof & structure clearing
  • Insurer documentation
06

Cabling, Bracing & Preservation

Support systems and preservation plans that extend the life of significant and heritage trees — dynamic cabling, lightning protection, and construction-site tree protection.

  • Dynamic cabling
  • Lightning protection
  • Heritage trees
About Summit

A tree company led by arborists, not salespeople.

Summit Tree Care was founded in 2009 on a simple conviction: trees are assets, and they deserve decisions made by people qualified to make them. Every estimate is performed by an ISA Certified Arborist who will tell you when a tree can be saved — even when saving it earns us less.

That honesty, paired with meticulous crews and complete site cleanup, is why the majority of our work arrives by referral from neighbors, property managers, and municipalities across the region.

How We Work

A considered process, from assessment to cleanup

No pressure, no obligation — a professional evaluation and a fixed, written proposal.

I.

Consultation

Call or write to us. We discuss your trees, your concerns, and schedule a site visit at a time that suits you.

II.

Arborist Assessment

A certified arborist inspects each tree's health, structure, and risk — and explains your options in plain language.

III.

Written Proposal

One fixed price, itemized by tree, with scope, method, and cleanup specified in writing. Valid for 30 days.

IV.

Execution & Restoration

Our crew completes the work to ANSI standards, removes all debris, and leaves your property immaculate.
